What we do

Three ways to work with us, depending on how far you want to go.

Start here

Cyber Essentials Readiness

Get ready for Cyber Essentials certification, without the guesswork.

  • Review your current setup against the five Cyber Essentials controls — firewalls, secure configuration, access control, malware protection and patch management
  • Give you a clear, prioritised list of what needs fixing before you apply
  • Guide you through the self-assessment questionnaire
  • Refer you to our certification body partner to sit the actual assessment

Go deeper

Cyber Security Maturity Assessment

An independent, evidence-based look at how your IT provider is really doing.

  • Interview your IT provider and review the evidence, not just their word
  • Map what we find against NIST CSF 2.0 and the CIS IG1 baseline controls
  • Score your governance, protections, detection and recovery capability
  • Deliver a plain-English report for the partners, with short and long-term priorities

Test it

Cyber Resilience Exercise

See how your firm actually responds when something goes wrong.

  • Run a 90-minute simulated incident with your leadership team — for example, a ransomware attack on a Friday afternoon
  • Test who gets called, what gets escalated, and whether your backups would really save you
  • Check your client notification and regulatory reporting obligations are understood
  • Follow up with a short action plan closing the gaps the exercise revealed
